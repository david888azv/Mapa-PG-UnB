About MAPA-PG

MAPA-PG — Monitoring and Analysis of Graduate Research Output — is an interactive system for following and analysing Brazilian graduate programmes across all 49 CAPES assessment fields. The base gathers every graduate programme in the country present in the CAPES data — 4,824 programmes at 495 institutions, spread over all 27 states. On opening, you choose one of the 27 federal universities as your reference university, which is then highlighted and compared with all its national peers in the same CAPES field.

The system lets you compare productivity indicators across programmes of every CAPES grade (3, 4, 5, 6 and 7), see trends over three four-year cycles (2013-2016, 2017-2020, 2021-2024), and generate exportable reports and charts.

How to use MAPA-PG

1. Filters (sidebar)

  • CAPES grades: tick grades 4, 5, 6 and/or 7 to filter the programmes
  • Cycle: choose a particular four-year cycle, or "All"
  • Metric: pick the productivity metric (mean articles per faculty member per year, by category)
  • Region: filter by geographic region (Central-West, Northeast, North, Southeast, South)
  • Output type: filter by type (journal article, abstract, conference paper, and so on)
  • Impact stratum: filter by impact-factor band (low/medium/high)
  • Patents / technical output: a button that opens the patents panel, with its own cycle selector — see the Patents section of this documentation

2. Results

  • Table: ranking of programmes with the full set of indicators
  • Charts: bars and trends over the cycles
  • Report: formatted text, ready to copy and analyse

3. Export

  • CSV: exports all filtered data as CSV (separator: semicolon)
  • PNG: exports every visible chart as a watermarked PNG image
  • Impact report: generates a detailed impact-factor report in a new window
  • Patents: the technical-output panel exports two CSVs — the aggregate by programme, and the full list of patents with title, registration number, dates and description

4. Things worth knowing

  • The reference university you chose is always highlighted in pink/red, in the charts and in the table
  • The reference does not filter the data: each field always shows every programme in the country. To open a field where it has no programme, tick “Show all 49 CAPES fields” in the sidebar
  • Click any column header in the table to sort by it
  • Faculty figures for 2022-2024 use forward-fill from the last available year (2021)
Programmes approved but not yet graded:
The CAPES catalogue marks with “A”, in place of a grade, the programmes approved through the new-programme route that have not yet been through a four-yearly assessment — 182 of them in the country, nearly all opened in 2024. Since every comparison in MAPA-PG rests on the grade, they do not enter means, rankings, charts or the reference-programme selector.
Since v5.6.0 they also do not vanish: they appear on the institution's opening screen, in the field label and in a note inside the panel, always identified as approved and not yet graded. Before that, a new programme simply did not exist anywhere in the application, which read easily as a programme that did not exist at all. Once CAPES publishes the grade, it is compared like any other.
A caveat on the number of faculty:
In the raw data published by the CAPES Open Data Portal, some graduate programmes show zero faculty in certain years, especially in the third cycle (2021-2024), whose faculty data were only published up to 2021. It can also happen occasionally in other cycles, for particular programmes.

To estimate output per researcher in those cases, MAPA-PG applies forward-fill: when a programme's faculty count is zero in a given year, the system uses the last non-zero record available for that programme instead. This keeps the productivity metrics (articles per faculty member per year) computable and consistent even where the faculty data are incomplete.

Values estimated this way should be read with care, since the real number of active researchers may have varied over the period.

Patents — technical output

Up to version 5.0, MAPA-PG measured only bibliographic output: journal articles, abstracts, conference papers, book chapters and newspaper pieces — the five subtypes CAPES publishes under the BIBLIOGRAPHIC type. Patents and other technological products sit in separate datasets of the Open Data Portal and were not part of the application. From v5.1 they are in, starting with the PATENT subtype; since v5.2, for all three cycles.

The panel

The "Patents / technical output" button in the sidebar opens a panel with: an overview of the field; the reference university's programme in the foreground, with patents per year and per permanent faculty member, its position in the field ranking and the list of patents (title, registration number, filing institution, dates, technology stage, research line and description); the ranking of the field's programmes; free-text search; and CSV export.

The panel's cycle selector is independent. It opens aligned with the sidebar's cycle filter when that points at a particular cycle, and at the most recent cycle with data when the filter is on "All". Changing cycle in the panel reloads the whole layer: each programme's patent count changes, because each cycle is a separate CAPES collection. Cycles in which the field reported no patent appear disabled.

Counting: declarations and distinct patents

A patent co-authored across programmes is reported by each participating programme. The count per programme uses the declaration — that is what CAPES considers when assessing that programme. The field and national totals, on the other hand, can only become "distinct patents" where the filing registration number exists to recognise the repetitions. And that field is not present in the same way across the three collections.

Reporting coverage by cycle

The fields are filled in by the programmes themselves on the Sucupira platform, and not all are mandatory — some do not even exist in every collection. National completion rates, over the total patent declarations of each cycle:

The three caveats that most affect how this reads:

1. In 2017-2020 there is no registration number. The collection simply does not publish the field. In that cycle there is no way to know how many declarations are the same patent reported by co-authoring programmes, so the panel shows only declarations and marks "distinct patents" as not determinable. In 2013-2016 the field exists, but filled in 40.9% of records: deduplication is partial and the distinct total still contains repetitions. Only in 2021-2024 is it reliable — there, 13,505 declarations correspond to 8,381 distinct patents, meaning about 38% of national declarations are repetition through co-authorship.

2. "Not collected" is not 0%. Where the table says not collected, the field did not exist in that collection's form — it is not that programmes failed to fill it in. The technology stage, for instance, was only asked for from 2017-2020 onwards. A blank field, in turn, means not reported, not that the fact is absent.

3. Comparing cycles takes care. A change in a programme's patent count between collections may reflect a change in reporting practice — or in the form — and not only a change in inventive activity. The count is of patents filed/reported in the cycle: the grant by the competent authority usually comes years later and is rarely reported (never above 11%).

One detail about the description field

The description comes from DS_FINALIDADE, a free-text field. In most cases it describes the invention, but in about 3% of the filled ones it carries only the asset category ("PATENTE DE INVENÇÃO", "REGISTRO DE CULTIVAR"). MAPA-PG separates the two cases: the category label becomes a tag on the patent card, and is not counted as a description.

10. Journals — CiteScore 2025 (Scopus / Elsevier)

O CiteScore é uma métrica bibliométrica de propriedade da Elsevier, calculada a partir da base de dados Scopus. É o indicador de impacto oficial prescrito pela Ficha de Avaliação CAPES 2025–2028 (junto ao JIF/JCR), e é a base padrão da estratificação A1–A8/C deste aplicativo (opção "Relatório Detalhado IF"; as bases OpenAlex e Híbrida também estão disponíveis).
