About MAPA-PG

MAPA-PG — Monitoring and Analysis of Graduate Research Output — is an interactive system for following and analysing Brazilian graduate programmes across all 49 CAPES assessment fields. The base gathers every graduate programme in the country present in the CAPES data — 4,824 programmes at 495 institutions, spread over all 27 states. On opening, you choose one of the 27 federal universities as your reference university, which is then highlighted and compared with all its national peers in the same CAPES field.

The system lets you compare productivity indicators across programmes of every CAPES grade (3, 4, 5, 6 and 7), see trends over three four-year cycles (2013-2016, 2017-2020, 2021-2024), and generate exportable reports and charts.

Public data: every figure used in this system comes from the CAPES Open Data Portal, part of the Brazilian federal government's transparency and freedom-of-information programme (Act no. 12.527/2011). The data are public and freely accessible. Download links for each file are in the "Data sources and URLs" tab.
A tailored edition for your institution. Besides the public version, we can build a bespoke edition for your programme, department or institution — public or private — aimed at a deeper, more detailed study of the indicators.

We are also available for consultancy on improving these indicators, grounded in rigorous analysis of the official data. We use advanced statistical methods — predictive, logistic and hierarchical models, among others — to identify and suggest the points that can be improved, with the aim of achieving better assessment results.

Requirements

  • A modern browser (Chrome, Firefox, Edge or Safari)
  • The manifest.json / registry.json catalogue and the file for the selected field (dados/area-*.json), in the same folder
  • An internet connection on first opening (to load Chart.js from the CDN)
