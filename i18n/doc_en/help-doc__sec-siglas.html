Acronym glossary — the Astronomy / Physics field as an example

MAPA-PG covers all 49 CAPES assessment fields; the list below is an example of one of them — the 66 programmes of the Astronomy / Physics field — with the institution's acronym, the programme name, the university and the starting year of its first course. Programme and institution names are kept in Portuguese, as they appear in the official data. In each field, UnB programmes get their own code in the form MAPA-PG-XXXXXX, shown in the sidebar selectors.

Source: Register of Graduate Programmes — CAPES Open Data. The starting year is the AN_INICIO_CURSO field (the first course recorded in the programme).
